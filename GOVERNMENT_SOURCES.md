## Accuracy review

Every bundled English and French exam question and answer was compared with the official delimited bank. Government reference titles, author metadata, document editions and page dates were checked against the linked originals. Key certificate, exam and station-identification rules were spot-checked; this is not a line-by-line legal review of every lesson.

- All 984 English and French questions, all four answers for each question, and the correct-answer positions match the live ISED delimited download.
- The 100 topic groups and source data SHA-256 match.
- The question-bank PDF cover was checked for the complete title, institutional author and effective date.
- All 12 government reference pages and ISED reproduction terms were opened. Their full titles, available author/creator metadata, editions and modification dates are recorded.
- Certificate/pass-mark rules, the 100-topic exam structure, the combined authorization and call-sign identification rules were checked against RIC-3, RIC-1, the certification page and RBR-4.

The report `government-source-verification.json` records the automated checks and the exact scope. Run `python3 scripts/verify_government_sources.py` with internet access before a future release. A changed or unavailable source causes a failed check; the script never rewrites lessons or questions.
